### Plan

**You own the plan, not the code. Interview, research, decide, draft, prototype, verify, hand off.** Delegate exploration, panels, and personas to leaf skills; stay in the lead. Templates, the Definition of Ready, the interview bank, and the reply rules are in this skill's `references/` and `playbooks/plan-interview.md`.

Size first (Bounded, Feature, Program per the mode's table) and say the budgets. Bounded fast path, by the step numbers below: step 2 asks only the frame questions the repo cannot answer (at most three), step 3 runs one explorer (more only when the substrate is unread, and say so), step 4 asks the remaining decision questions (at most two more) and presents premises and stories as one gate, step 5 skips the arena, step 6 is skipped unless a UX fork appears, step 7 is the tech lead plus at most one seat by signal (the pm panel and personas run only on request, and the Definition of Ready item for them reads "not cast, Bounded"). Program routes to `playbooks/program.md`.

1. Intake. If the request names an existing folder under `docs/plans/`, run `playbooks/revise.md` instead. Otherwise, in this order. (a) Baseline: apply the profile's rule, record the SHA and the behind-count, and run the mode's tree check; a stale checkout means the probe and every anchor read `origin/<default>`, and "which tree?" is asked before anything else. (b) Probe, read-only, against the default branch and the remotes, never the checkout alone: `git grep -il <nouns> origin/<default> -- <source dirs>`, `git ls-tree -r --name-only origin/<default> | grep -i <nouns>`, `git branch -r | grep -i <nouns>`, `git log --all --oneline -i --grep=<nouns> | head`, `git worktree list`, the flag helper's names, every ledger under `docs/plans/`, and `.product-builder/learnings.md`. It returns three lines that open the ledger header: whether the ask already ships (`ships`, `partial`, or `absent`, with the file:line or the exact search that failed), the closest existing feature, and the tree the anchors are read from. A design doc or spec found on a branch or in another worktree is read and cited as prior art; a plan folder on the same area is cited as a prior attempt and its locked and rejected decisions are read before any question; a learning that touches the area is cited as "prior learning" when it shapes a call. (c) If the probe says `ships`, stop and ask one question with the options (nothing to do; a different ask; a v2 on top of what shipped); that question is the whole reply. (d) Size, marked provisional, from the probe's three lines rather than the phrasing; restated at step 3. (e) Choose a slug (kebab, at most four words). Create `docs/plans/<slug>/decisions.md` from the template with status Draft, the size, the budgets, and the probe's three lines. Every decision the user stated in the conversation before the suite was invoked ("one PR", "a toggle", "leave X alone") is a D-row with Who `user` at intake, quoted; the interview never re-asks one, and the question budget counts only the exit items still missing. An artifact this session produced before the suite ran (a design doc, a draft build in the working tree) is cited as a prior attempt and, before Ready, either folded into the plan folder or deleted; the ledger records which. Restate the ask in two lines.
2. Interview 1 (frame). Before each question, check whether the repo answers it (grep, git log, docs under the plan root, `.product-builder/personas.md`, and any other folder under `docs/plans/` that touches the area, cited as a prior attempt). A question turn is at most twelve lines before the question; supporting evidence is three bullets at most, the rest lives in the ledger. When the user has stated a number or a preset, quote it back verbatim in the recommendation; when a recommendation changes something the user said, say so in the same sentence. Pick at most eight questions from the bank in `playbooks/plan-interview.md`, one per reply, each with a recommended answer and its reason; use the question tool when the answers are enumerable. Exit when these are recorded: problem statement (at most three sentences), today's workaround, one to three persona seeds, appetite, at least one non-goal, success signal and kill criterion, constraints, prior attempts, stakeholders. An exit item the conversation or the repo already answers is recorded with its source and not asked. Write the draft `product.md` (Problem, Who, Appetite, Non-goals, Success signal) and ledger rows. Close by stating the three to five premises the plan rests on (see `plan-interview.md`) and present them with the framing as a short block; wait for agreement: this is G1, a product call.
3. Research. Invoke the **product-builder-research** skill for the slug. Read its "Closest existing feature", "What this changes about the framing", and "New questions for Interview 2". Present at most five bullets of what changed. Restate the size with the research in hand, up or down, and say which budgets changed; a downgrade from Program removes the parts skeleton. Ask one question only if a framing item is contradicted; otherwise continue. Status Researched.
4. Interview 2 (decide) plus stories and assumptions. Build the decision tree from the research questions, the product forks (scope, terminology against the glossary, defaults, permissions per role, visibility, empty and error states and their copy, destructive actions and undo, notifications, mobile, flag-off behaviour) and the technical forks (seam, data model, sync or async, migration, backward compatibility, integration points, flag, instrumentation). Depth-first, dependencies first. Ask product calls one per reply with a default. Recommend technical calls and record them; ask only when the call changes what the user sees or costs appetite. Never ask the user to lock a decision that depends on a mechanism research has not yet read (a permission rule that an existing pattern might make unnecessary, a job that a query-time predicate might replace); read the mechanism first, then ask, or mark the decision `pending research` and come back to it. A fork an experiment can settle is marked `→ prototype` or `→ spike`, never asked. Every behavioural clause in a decision ("re-run when the task changes materially", "only when the user has not pinned it") names how the system detects the condition and what that costs, read from the code or the changes table, before the decision locks; a clause with no mechanism is `pending research` or is cut, and a decision the implementation later has to drop is a plan defect, not an implementation one. Draft three to eight stories (Bounded: one to three) in the format in `plan-interview.md`; present the set as one numbered list for editing, then discuss contested stories one at a time. Every must-story is demoable alone, carries a failure line, names its surface and five states (UI) or consumer and contract sample (non-UI), and a permissions line where roles exist. Before presenting the set, read each story against the decisions it rests on and fix any line that widens or narrows a locked decision. Ask which story is cut first if the appetite runs out. List the assumptions (desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, ethical) by importance and evidence; each riskiest one gets a test (prototype, persona, spike, data, one question). Wait for the story set: this is G3.
5. Spikes, then the approach arena, drafts, appetite check. First, run every fork and assumption marked `→ spike` in step 4 through **product-builder-research** `--spikes` now, before any architect is dispatched; a high-importance assumption with no verdict after its spike blocks G4, and one whose spike cannot run here is recorded as an accepted risk with the reason or blocks; `pending` does not survive this step. Feature and Program: dispatch two or three architect subagents in parallel with the same stories, research, and canon and distinct mandates (minimal change; clean shape; pragmatic), each returning a usage sketch, files touched, slice order, what it gives up. Compare in a table, recommend one, ask only if candidates differ in what the user sees or in appetite. When the chosen approach rests on a mechanism claim about existing code (a reducer restores state, a token blocks a refetch), prove it with a ten-line test or script before the tech lead runs; a mechanism that fails the spike sends the approach back to the arena. Write `product.md` (about two pages) and `implementation.md` (about three) from the templates: current-to-target diagram with every current box anchored in `research.md`; approach naming the closest feature modelled on; simpler alternatives with the story each fails; changes table with verified paths; data and contracts; test strategy; observability; slices one PR each, riskiest first, P1 the flag plus skeleton when a flag exists, a schema expand its own first slice, each with stories, files, data, flag, tests per acceptance line named by acceptance id (`S1-a`, `S2-b`) rather than by a test title, live check, regression, rollback, size. Add up the slices against the appetite; if over, propose cuts (drop later stories, defer slices, shrink to the must-line) and ask. Everything cut or deferred is written to the ledger's Deferred section with what would bring it back; a deferral that is not written down does not exist. Coverage: every story to a slice, every slice to a story or an enabling reason. Self-review: no placeholders; every story line agrees with the decision it rests on (a story that widens or narrows a locked decision is a bug, fix it before presenting); no unanchored claims; caps held. Show each doc as its path plus a ten-line digest and the appetite verdict, then continue: G4 presents.
6. Prototype, only if a decision is marked `→ prototype`, a story's acceptance depends on feel, or the surface is new. Otherwise write `Prototype: none (<reason>)` in the header and `skip:` the step. Invoke the **product-builder-prototype** skill. G5 presents the recommendation and continues.
7. Verify. Invoke **product-builder-personas** (prototype target, or paper mode against the stories), **product-builder-pm** on `product.md`, and **product-builder-techlead** on `implementation.md`. Show the findings as one block (at most eight lines, grouped by bucket, with source and confidence). Apply the reversible edits and list them. A ruling that cuts scope or changes a product call is asked and the reply ends there; a decision marked ASSUMED is listed as such and never described as "waiting on you" in a reply that continues. Then reconcile: read `product.md` and `implementation.md` against the rulings table and every superseded row; a section that still describes an overruled decision (a sentinel the panel killed, a path the census split in two) is edited and the edit listed, and that list is the evidence for the Definition of Ready's reconciliation box. Re-run only changed stories and slices. Exit when no act-on is open. Status Verified.
8. Definition of Ready. Print the list from `references/templates.md` in the reply, each box with the evidence that checks it (a decision id, a spike verdict row, a test name, a file:line, a rulings-table count); a box without evidence is red, and a missing item sends the plan back to the step that owns it. "Green" is never asserted without the printed list; a list that is not in the reply has not run. When green: status Ready to implement, revision-log row "initial plan". Offer tickets per slice if the profile has a tracker. Wait for "go": G7.

**Reply:** the plan folder path; who the feature is for and what changes for them; the digests of `product.md` and `implementation.md`; the approach chosen and the candidates it beat, in a table; the principles that shaped the calls; what verification found and what was applied; ASSUMED rows first, then open questions with owners; the prototype path; the next move ("go" starts `playbooks/implement.md`).
