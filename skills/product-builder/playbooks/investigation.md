### Investigation

**You own the answer. Route, read, write.** Read-only. The deliverable is a cited explanation or a recommendation, not a change.

1. Invoke the **product-builder-how** skill with the Skill tool, even for a one-module question (it runs one explainer for those); do not do its reading inline and do not open its file with Read. For motivation questions ("why is it like this", "who decided"), also the **product-builder-why** skill.
2. For "A or B", produce a recommendation with a trade-offs table and your real judgment.
3. The reply follows the how shape (Overview, Key concepts, How it works, Where things live, Gotchas), every claim with file:line at the baseline SHA or a label (measured, inferred, guess).
4. No PR, no change. If a change follows, hand back and match Bug fix or Plan.

**Reply:** the investigation output. For "are we sure" the judgment with reasons. Push back if the premise is wrong.
