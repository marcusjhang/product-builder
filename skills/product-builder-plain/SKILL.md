Restate your last message. Drop the jargon, keep every fact and every number with the meaning it had, one idea per sentence, as one person talking to another, and shorter than the original. A term whose meaning you are not sure of stays in quotes rather than being guessed at: "the probe returned partial" means part of the feature already exists, not that the reading was incomplete. The writing rules in `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../product-builder/references/writing.md` apply: no long dashes, no praise, no closing offer.
